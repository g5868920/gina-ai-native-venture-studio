## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Approved | Explicitly accepted as the current product or governance direction |
| Working direction | Preferred path to test; not yet validated or irreversible |
| Pending | Requires founder, user, technical, legal, or market decision |
| Superseded | Previously valid but replaced by a newer approved direction |
| Rejected | Explicitly excluded from the intended product direction |
