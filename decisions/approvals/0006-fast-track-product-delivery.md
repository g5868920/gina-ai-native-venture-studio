## Decision

The Chairwoman approves the Fast-Track Product Delivery reprioritization:

1. **AI-Native PM Workflow is the primary active priority** — deliver the demo-ready Control Tower prototype in its independent product repository, per `handoffs/outbound/pm-workflow-control-tower-execution-handoff.md`.
2. **Twinko is the secondary active priority** — prepare and deliver the iOS-first UI prototype in its independent product repository, per `handoffs/outbound/twinko-ui-prototype-preparation-handoff.md`.
3. **PM Workflow dogfooding and Twinko Discovery-to-Decision are deferred until both prototypes are ready.** They are **deferred, not superseded or rejected** — this resolves the sequencing tension surfaced in `memory/milestones/2026-07-12-fast-track-product-delivery.md` (venture 7-day priorities and Twinko D-047 ordering): those tracks resume after prototype delivery.
4. **Product repositories own implementation and product assets.** The Studio owns cross-venture priorities, milestone tracking, major decisions, risks and blockers, product-repo handoffs, and portfolio evidence.
5. **Twinko visual and motion assets remain in the Twinko product repo.** The Studio tracks only references, status, risks and handoffs — resolving the asset-location question raised in the Twinko preparation handoff.
6. **No new Studio infrastructure unless it directly blocks product delivery.**

## Scope and Limits

Sets portfolio priority and boundaries only. Does not decide Twinko wedge, first-value moment, naming, scope freeze, or any other pending product decision; does not modify either product repository's canonical truth; does not authorize commits, releases or external communication. Evidence rules stand: prototypes are demo layers, and no external commercial-validation claim may be made from them.

Chairwoman directive of 2026-07-12; `memory/milestones/2026-07-12-fast-track-product-delivery.md`; venture handoffs (2026-07-11); `CLAUDE.md`; prior approvals 0001–0005.
