## Decision

The Chairwoman approves the Phase 2A CEO design v0.1, comprising the canonical CEO files:

- `executives/ceo/OPERATING_CONTRACT.md` — CEO mission, authority, accountabilities, inputs/outputs, operating modes, decision-packet and handoff rules, cadence, success measures, failure modes, completion/writeback boundaries;
- `executives/ceo/LEADERSHIP_PROFILE.md` — five leadership behavior models (Bezos, Grove, Nadella, Netflix, selective first-principles acceleration) as behavior libraries, with anti-pattern guardrails;
- `skills/ceo/consulting-proposal-review/SKILL.md` — consulting proposal review mindset: diagnose first, facts vs. assumptions, options and trade-offs, scope/out-of-scope, deliverables, adoption, maintenance, governance, no unsupported ROI claims.

## Scope and Limits

This decision does **not**: approve CPO/CTO design; create registries, agents, APIs, model calls, or orchestration; alter reserved decisions, the status model, or `constitution/AUTHORITY.md`; authorize any commit, release, or external communication. Leadership references remain behavior libraries — impersonation of real leaders stays prohibited.
